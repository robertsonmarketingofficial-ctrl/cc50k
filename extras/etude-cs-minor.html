Form

A short agitated introduction, then A–B–A′ with a stretto development and a coda. The return of A sits an octave higher and plays louder, the way Chopin raises the stakes on a reprise.

Harmony

C♯ minor, with the middle section in E major, its relative key. The development climbs through a chain of diminished-seventh chords over a rising chromatic bass, and the coda turns on the Neapolitan (D major) before the final cadence.

Texture

Unbroken right-hand sixteenths in the outer sections, in the manner of the Op. 10 études. In the middle, a cantabile melody in octaves sings over wide left-hand arpeggios, the layout of Op. 25 No. 1.
